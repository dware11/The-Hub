# C.O.D.E. Hub V2 backlog

## People and access invitations

- Let explicitly authorized staff add or invite people from Workspace.
- Require PVAMU email identity; a student-organization email may be attached only as a secondary contact.
- Support proactive user addition without waiting for a contributor request.
- Separate the future `Can Add People` permission from `Can Change Roles`.
- Reviewer access alone must never imply role-management authority.
- Give limited inviters a dedicated contributor-invite/add-person page without access to the full People & Access directory.
- `Can Add People` may invite a PVAMU identity as a Contributor, but must not permit changing roles, promoting users, or managing existing accounts.
- Require the invitee's PVAMU email as the core identity. Store a student-organization email only as optional secondary metadata.
- Reuse the existing email infrastructure with the message: “You’ve been invited to join the C.O.D.E. Engineering Hub.”
- Admins and Super Admins may receive invite capability as part of their broader access; implementation remains deferred to V2.

## Published-content update requests

- Let contributors propose changes to published content without directly editing the public record.
- Support deadline, date/time, location, application/registration link, cancellation/closure, and other corrections.
- Preserve the published version until an authorized reviewer approves the proposed update.
- Keep the request, reviewer decision, prior values, and resulting values in the audit history.
